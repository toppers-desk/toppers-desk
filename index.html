Science • Chapter: Light (5-Mark Expected)

Q1. Write the rules for image formation by a Concave Mirror with ray diagrams.

  • Rule 1 (Parallel Ray): A ray parallel to the principal axis passes through the principal focus (F) after reflection.
  • Rule 2 (Focus Ray): A ray passing through the principal focus (F) becomes parallel to the principal axis after reflection.
  • Rule 3 (Center Ray): A ray passing through the center of curvature (C) reflects back along the exact same path.
  • Rule 4 (Pole Ray): A ray incident obliquely at the pole (P) reflects obliquely making equal angles with the principal axis.
Exam Hall Tip: JKBOSE examiners cut 1.5 marks if arrows are missing on rays. Always draw sharp pencil arrows showing incoming and reflected light!
